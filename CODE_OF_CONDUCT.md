# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately at the address listed in the repository's profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, which applies in full.
